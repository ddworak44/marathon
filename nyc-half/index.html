Effort guide

Easy and long
Conversational. Keep recovery runs at the gentlest end of that effort.
Strides
Quick, relaxed 15–20 second accelerations with full easy recovery.
Threshold
Controlled and comfortably hard, around the effort you could hold for an hour. Start slower than your 5K pace and finish feeling like you could do one more rep.
10K effort
Use a recent 10K pace. If you only have a 5K result, start between that pace and threshold effort and keep the reps even.
Half effort
Use the pace you can sustain for 13.1 miles. Before the 10K benchmark, use a controlled effort a little below threshold.

Workout mileage includes warm-up, recovery jogs and cool-down. Start harder sessions with 10–15 minutes easy and finish with about 10 minutes easy. Practice race fueling on the long runs.

If your recent weeks are closer to 40 mi, shorten the easy runs or take Saturday off to trim 4–6 mi in the bigger weeks. Start Friday long runs at a distance you’ve handled recently and build toward 14–15 mi as recovery allows.

If you’re still recovering from the marathon, keep the early runs easy and use your current mileage. Once the harder sessions start, keep an easy day or rest day after each one. NYRR training guidance
